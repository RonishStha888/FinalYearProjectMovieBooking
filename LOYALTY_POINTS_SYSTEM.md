# 🎁 RTX Cinema Loyalty Points System

## Overview

Reward your customers with loyalty points for every ticket purchase! Points can be redeemed for discounts on future bookings.

---

## 🎯 How It Works

### Earning Points
- **1 Ticket = 10 Points**
- **Rs. 100 spent = 5 Points**
- **Bonus:** First booking = 50 bonus points
- **Bonus:** 5+ tickets in one booking = 25 bonus points

### Redeeming Points
- **100 Points = Rs. 50 discount**
- **200 Points = Rs. 100 discount**
- **500 Points = Rs. 300 discount**
- **1000 Points = Rs. 700 discount**

### Point Tiers
- **Bronze** (0-499 points): Standard benefits
- **Silver** (500-999 points): 5% extra points on bookings
- **Gold** (1000-1999 points): 10% extra points + priority booking
- **Platinum** (2000+ points): 15% extra points + exclusive offers

---

## 📊 Features

1. **Points Dashboard** - View total points, tier, and history
2. **Earn on Every Booking** - Automatic points calculation
3. **Redeem at Checkout** - Apply points for instant discount
4. **Points History** - Track all earned and redeemed points
5. **Tier Benefits** - Unlock rewards as you earn more
6. **Expiry System** - Points expire after 1 year (optional)

---

## 💰 Example Scenarios

### Scenario 1: First Time User
- Books 2 tickets @ Rs. 300 each = Rs. 600
- Earns: 20 points (2 tickets) + 30 points (Rs. 600 spent) + 50 bonus = **100 points**
- Next booking: Can redeem for Rs. 50 discount!

### Scenario 2: Regular Customer
- Has 450 points (Bronze tier)
- Books 3 tickets @ Rs. 300 each = Rs. 900
- Earns: 30 points (3 tickets) + 45 points (Rs. 900 spent) = **75 points**
- Total: 525 points → **Upgraded to Silver tier!**

### Scenario 3: Loyal Customer
- Has 1200 points (Gold tier)
- Books 2 tickets @ Rs. 300 each = Rs. 600
- Earns: 20 points + 30 points = 50 points
- Gold bonus: +10% = 5 extra points
- Total earned: **55 points**
- Redeems 200 points for Rs. 100 discount
- Final: 1055 points remaining
