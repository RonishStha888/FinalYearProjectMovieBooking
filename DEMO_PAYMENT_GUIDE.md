## 🚀 How to Demo Payment Right Now

### Option 1: Credit/Debit Card Payment (Recommended for Demo)

1. **Select Movie & Seats**
   - Go to http://localhost:5173
   - Login with your account
   - Select a movie, date, time, cinema
   - Choose your seats (10-minute timer starts!)
   - Add F&B items (optional)

2. **Go to Payment Page**
   - You'll see 3 payment options:
     - 💳 Credit/Debit Card
     - 📱 eSewa
     - 💜 Khalti (skip this for now)

3. **Select "Credit/Debit Card"**

4. **Enter ANY Card Details** (they won't be validated):
   ```
   Card Number: 4111 1111 1111 1111
   Expiry Date: 12/25
   CVV: 123
   Cardholder Name: YOUR NAME
   ```

5. **Click "Pay Rs. XXX"**
   - Processing animation shows (3 seconds)
   - Payment completes successfully!

6. **See Your Ticket!**
   - Booking reference generated
   - Ticket displayed with QR code
   - Email confirmation sent
   - Loyalty points awarded

### Option 2: eSewa Payment

1. Follow steps 1-2 above
2. **Select "eSewa"**
3. **Click "Pay Rs. XXX"**
   - Simulated eSewa payment
   - Completes in 3 seconds
   - Same result as card payment!
